What changed (src/AdminWeb/src)
- App.tsx: "Skip to main content" link first in the page; <main id="main-content" tabIndex=-1>;
  menu button aria-expanded + aria-controls; opening the phone menu moves focus to its first link;
  Escape closes it and returns focus to the button; load-error panel role="alert".
- styles.css: skip link visible only on focus; on phones the closed menu gets visibility:hidden
  (after the slide-out transition), so its links leave the Tab order.
- AdminExperience.tsx: quick jump keeps Tab / Shift+Tab inside the dialog, returns focus to where it
  was on close, and drops role="listbox" (its children were buttons, not options).
- 17 dynamic error banners get role="alert"; the "Live progress delayed" notice and 9 success
  banners get role="status" (polite).
